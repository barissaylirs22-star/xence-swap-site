Slide 01 · Intro

Solana routing, simplified into one token.

Routing-first design

AXM is designed as a routing & access token that can sit on top of existing liquidity. The UI stays focused on swaps while the routing logic stays under the hood.

Utility beyond swaps

Over time, AXM can plug into access passes, fee tiers, integrations and partner flows that want a lightweight routing token on Solana.

Front-door into the ecosystem

The goal is simple: one clean URL where users can see the token, check the docs, connect wallets and reach trusted liquidity.
Slide 02 · Why AXM

Why AXM exists.

Jupiter-powered routing

AXM can use curated routes and liquidity across Solana DEXs while keeping the surface simple. The engine can evolve; the token front door stays stable.

Simple front-door UX

Users should not need to hunt across different URLs or tokens. Xence Swap focuses on a single, minimal interface with AXM at the center.

Integration-ready utility

As AXM grows, partners and on-chain programs can treat AXM as the “routing key” for rewards, access tiers and integrations.

Design notes

AXM is designed as a fixed-supply experiment on Solana. Allocation can evolve as the project matures, but the goal is to keep things transparent and simple for early users.

Exact on-chain allocations and wallet labels are visible on Solscan. Any future changes to supply or strategy will be communicated publicly.
