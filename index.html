Slide 02 · Why AXM exists

Why build a separate routing & utility token?

AXM is not just another meme coin. It is a routing token on top of Jupiter that can plug into dashboards, bots and front-ends which all share the same liquidity.

  • Jupiter-powered routing.  Orders are routed through Solana’s top DEXs via Jupiter, giving you best execution and low slippage.
  • Single front-door UX.  Xence Swap keeps the UX clean while the heavy routing logic lives inside Jupiter and partner integrations.
  • Integration-ready utility.  AXM can plug into yield flows, fee rebates, access tiers and more, depending on how the community evolves the project.
Think of AXM as a “router assistant” on Solana.

Assistant program · quick highlights

Liquidity-backed price
Routed liquidity from Solana DEXs ensures AXM trades reference deep on-chain books instead of isolated pools.
Composable incentives
Volume, LPs and integrators can all be rewarded with AXM over time, wired via simple on-chain programs.
Integrator-friendly
Dashboards, bots and apps can embed the same swap widget and still route through AXM.
Experiment, not empire
The goal is to prove a clean routing-token design, not to become a mega-protocol that tries to do everything.

Slide 04 · How to get AXM

Three quick steps to join the pool.

If you are new to Solana or Raydium, this is the short version of how to get started with AXM.

  1. Install a Solana wallet (Phantom recommended) and fund it with a small amount of SOL.
  2. Visit the Raydium link below and swap SOL (or another supported asset via Jupiter) into AXM.
  3. Optionally, add liquidity to the SOL / AXM pool and earn trading fees on top of price exposure.
Always keep enough SOL for network fees.

Slide 05 · Tokenomics

Simple, transparent AXM distribution.

No 20-page spreadsheet circus. AXM keeps the allocation model straightforward so holders can see where the supply lives.

  • Fixed supply.  Mint authority is disabled, freeze authority is off. What’s minted is what exists.
  • Liquidity first.  A dedicated chunk of AXM is reserved for Raydium liquidity and market making.
  • Program-driven growth.  Treasury, marketing and airdrops are designed to support routing usage, not random hype.
