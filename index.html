Why does AXM exist?

AXM is the routing & utility token for a Solana-first swap front-end. It is designed as an experiment in building a lightweight “liquidity access layer” on top of existing DEX infrastructure.

  • Routing token on top of Jupiter. Xence Swap focuses on UX while all heavy pricing and path finding lives inside Jupiter.
  • Aligned with Solana fees & liquidity. No custom AMM, no extra risk. AXM sits on top of the best available routes.
  • Room for integrations. Over time, yields, fee rebates and partner products can plug into AXM routing volume.
Jupiter-powered routing Single front-door UX Integration-ready

AXM assistant program · highlights

Think of AXM as the “assistant layer” for routing flows: a token that can be wired into dashboards, bots, and products that build on top of Jupiter.

  • Liquidity-backed price discovery. Pricing is derived from top Solana DEXs via Jupiter. AXM does not create its own AMM; it amplifies existing liquidity.
  • Composable incentives. Volume, LPs and integrators can all receive AXM-based rewards defined by governance or programmatic rules.
  • Integrator-friendly. Dashboards, bots and front-ends can embed the same swap widget and still route through AXM.

Roadmap

AXM is deliberately lean. The roadmap focuses on shipping real integrations, not endless promises.

  • Phase 1 · Launch & base liquidity. Token deployed on Solana, Raydium pool initialized, Jupiter routing wired into xence-swap.xyz.
  • Phase 2 · Community & analytics. Basic dashboards, on-chain stats, small targeted airdrops to early swappers and LPs.
  • Phase 3 · Integrations & tooling. Simple SDK / embed snippets so other sites can plug AXM routing into their front-ends.
