Slide 02 · Why AXM exists

Solana routing, simplified into one token.

Under the hood, AXM can plug into Solana’s major venues and route liquidity while the front-end stays clean. The goal is simple: less clicking around, more predictable fills.

Routing-first design
AXM is designed as a routing & access token that can sit on top of existing liquidity. The UI stays focused on swaps while the routing logic lives under the hood.
Utility beyond swaps
Over time, AXM can plug into access passes, fee tiers, integrations and partner flows that want a lightweight routing token on Solana.
Front-door into the ecosystem
The goal is simple: one clean URL where users can see the token, check docs, connect wallets and reach trusted liquidity.
